02 / Embroidery

Thread with depth.

Embroidery turns a mark into texture. It is a tactile, structured finish for the pieces people reach for every day: caps, polos, jackets, bags and workwear.

Thread / depth / detailMA2K / 02

The method

Give the logo a surface people can feel.

Embroidery is about structure: the way the thread catches light, the way a small mark gains authority and the way a garment feels more finished because the design is built into it. We help simplify artwork where needed so the final stitch stays clean.

Best for

  • Caps, beanies and headwear
  • Polos, jackets and workwear
  • Company and team uniforms
  • Small marks and lettering

Think about

  • Stitch count and level of detail
  • Thread color and garment contrast
  • Placement on structured garments
  • How the item will be worn and washed

The embroidery feeling

Quiet detail. Strong identity.

Embroidery does not need to shout. A well-placed mark, stitched with restraint, can make a uniform feel like a brand and a blank feel like a keepsake.

Built to be worn

A considered finish

From first thread to final piece.

We keep the path simple: review the art, choose the garment, settle the placement and make sure the stitch supports the design.
