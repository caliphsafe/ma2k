# MA2K Product Media Sources

This patch replaces generic/repeated product art with a curated local image for every catalog item.

## Supplier-authorized media

Most sign, display, graphic, decal, vehicle and rigid-substrate images are cropped from the publicly available Signs365 supplier catalog. MA2K confirmed its supplier relationship and permission to use supplier media in customer-facing sales materials. Images were cropped and optimized for web use.
