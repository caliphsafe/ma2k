REQUEST QUOTE

Use when the job needs review.

Choose this for custom fabrication, unusual dimensions, installation, freight, complex artwork, variable quantities, or any project where MA2K should confirm production details before the customer pays.

  • Customer submits specifications and artwork
  • MA2K reviews production requirements
  • A final quote is approved before payment

ORDER ONLINE

Use when pricing is complete and predictable.

Choose this only for standardized products with dependable material, size, finishing, shipping, and pricing rules. Customers can configure the product and proceed to Square payment.

  • Pricing formula must be complete
  • Required options must be supported
  • Square must be connected

RECOMMENDED WORKFLOW

Start conservatively, then enable online ordering.

Keep new or complicated products on Request Quote. Move a product to Order Online only after testing its pricing formula, minimum charge, artwork requirements, and fulfillment process.

HOW IT WORKS

A secure authorization flow.

  1. Select Connect Square.
  2. Sign in on Square’s secure website.
  3. Choose the correct MA2K Square account.
  4. Approve the requested permissions.
  5. Return here and confirm the connected merchant.

The application secret and payment credentials remain server-side. MA2K administrators should never paste a Square access token into a browser form.

ONLINE ORDER READINESS

Square connection is one part of launch.

A product should be marked Order Online only when its pricing formula, production options, taxes, shipping or pickup rules, and customer artwork flow have all been tested.
