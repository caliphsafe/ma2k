Our process

Clear from the first question
to the final pickup.

A custom project has many moving parts. Our job is to organize them into a process that feels understandable and professional.

The principleNo production without clear details and approval.
01
Start

Tell us what you are making.

Share the item, quantity, deadline, artwork and any examples that help explain the direction.

02
Recommendation

We match the idea to the method.

Screen printing, embroidery, vinyl or another service is recommended based on the actual project—not a default package.

03
Confirmation

The details become a production plan.

Garment, colors, placement, sizing, timing and other specifications are organized and confirmed.

04
Approval

You approve before production.

The project does not move into production until the direction is understood and approved.

05
Completion

We produce, check and prepare pickup.

The finished order is reviewed and prepared for the agreed delivery or pickup plan.

Ready for step one?

Start with what you know. We will help with the rest.
