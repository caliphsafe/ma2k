Family owned since 1996

SouthCoast craft, built on family values.

MA2K Impression is a family-owned and operated full-service screen-printing and embroidery company located in New Bedford, Massachusetts. Since 1996, we have helped businesses, schools, teams, organizations, events and independent creators turn ideas into apparel and promotional materials people are proud to use and wear.

Our family values and work ethic shape every part of the experience—from listening carefully and helping customers choose the right production method to checking the final pieces before they leave the shop. We believe dependable communication, honest guidance and consistent craftsmanship are just as important as the finished product.
