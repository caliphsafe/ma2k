Built in screen printing. Expanded for everything your brand needs.

A full-service production partner rooted in New Bedford.

MA2K Impression is a family-owned and operated commercial printing, signage, apparel-decoration and promotional-products company in New Bedford, Massachusetts. We began with screen printing and have grown to help businesses, schools, teams, organizations, events and individuals manage complete visual programs through one trusted partner.

Since 1996, our family values and work ethic have shaped how we approach every order: listen carefully, recommend the right process, communicate clearly and stand behind the finished work.

One coordinated partner

Local pickup and delivery

Shipping beyond the SouthCoast

Orders can be prepared for shipment throughout the United States and internationally, depending on product size and destination.
