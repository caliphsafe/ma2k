# Signs365 supplier media patch

This patch adds product imagery from the publicly accessible Signs365 signage catalog to:
- homepage product cards
- product catalog cards
- all 39 product pages
- reusable page-background image folders

The public catalog does not expose the authenticated "How To Videos" library. Add any supplier-approved MP4 files to `assets/videos/products/` after obtaining them from the Signs365 account or supplier representative. Do not hotlink protected portal assets.
